FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is AIU?

AIU is your personal AI assistant for business. Unlike chatbots that forget you instantly, AIU remembers your business, your style, and your goals — and gets better after every conversation. It builds lead funnels, websites, and apps, generates spokesperson videos and ad creatives, captures and manages your leads, and answers everyday questions like a strategist.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a great general chatbot, but it doesn't know your business, can't build and publish funnels, can't generate videos, and doesn't track your leads. AIU is an assistant with tools and memory — it does the work, not just the talking.

Do I need tech skills?

No. If you can describe your business in a sentence, you can use AIU. The assistant asks clarifying questions and builds everything for you — pages, copy, videos, the lot.

What do I get with Lifetime access?

Every feature, every future update, forever — for one payment of $1,999. That includes 300 AI requests per day, 15 AI videos per month, and 100 studio generations per month.

How do monthly plans and trials work?

Can I cancel? Do you refund?

You can cancel anytime from the Billing section in your account. All sales are final — we don't offer refunds, so cancel before your trial or billing period ends if you don't want to be charged. See the Refund Policy below.
